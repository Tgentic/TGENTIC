What the report carries

GET /audit/report?agent=0x… · tgentic/audit-report/1 · signed by 0x4faa…dff225

  • financialsCounted score, raw reputation, receipts, unique payers, locked bond — read from the Receipts contract, not from us.
  • riskManipulation heuristics over the receipt trail: payer concentration, timing regularity, amount cycling. The same detector as /risk.
  • subject identityThe agent's signed manifest — name, endpoints, agentId, manifestHash — verified against the rotation chain.
  • auditor attestationWho signed it — name, agent, agentId — plus a plain-language verdict and the issuance timestamp.
  • signatureAn ECDSA signature over the report's canonical bytes. Recover the signer offline — it is the auditor, or the report is worthless.
